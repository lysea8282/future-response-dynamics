# Section 4.1 figure caption

Release-wise fixed-entry occupancy and transported-image capture on the 1,024-unit carrier-fit population. The intervention-induced correction rapidly leaves the original rank-4 entry plane, while its trajectory-specific transported image continues to capture most of the later correction across the rollout. Curves show checkpoint-specific medians for 291402-291404.

Panels A and B use identical 0-1 fraction scales. The population is the reused carrier-fit pool, not a fresh confirmatory replication. No uncertainty bands are shown. The curves report medians and do not imply that every unit is fully captured or that the rank-4 interface is a sufficient recurrent state.
