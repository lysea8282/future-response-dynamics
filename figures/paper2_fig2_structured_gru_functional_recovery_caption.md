Figure 2. Tangent-restart recovery remains high across the baseline-eligible structured-GRU checkpoints, while two cells fail the stricter complete specificity assay. Each point shows the checkpoint-stratum median normalized recovery on the fixed 64-unit confirmation registry, with S1 and S2 shown separately. Filled markers denote cells that pass the complete assay; open markers denote cells that retain transport/function support but fail contrastability or specificity (291405/S1 and 291413/S2). The dashed line at recovery = 1 marks equality with the operational patched route under the same error metric and is not a pass threshold. Across the nine baseline-eligible checkpoints, all 18 cells satisfy the transport-capture and functional criteria; recovery spans 0.744-1.034, with 17/18 cells above 0.85, while the complete assay passes 16/18 cells. Checkpoint 291414 is not plotted because it fails the baseline-health criterion.
